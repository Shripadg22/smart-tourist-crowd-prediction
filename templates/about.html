ABOUT THE PROJECT

Tourism planning, made clearer.

This project helps users compare estimated crowd intensity across destinations and time slots.

01

Problem

Tourists may not have a single view comparing crowd conditions at different times.

02

Proposed solution

Combine destination information, date/time selection, estimates, recommendations, and charts.

03

Technology

Python Flask, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Chart.js, and SQLite for local prediction history.

Important limitation

Current percentages are illustrative rule-based estimates, not live crowd measurements or a trained machine-learning forecast. A genuine model needs reliable historical visitor-count data and testing.
